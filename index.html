Postdoctoral Fellow
Department of Data and Systems Engineering
The University of Hong Kong

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Max Z.J. Shen and Prof. Shaochong Lin. I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from HKU in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Jintao Ke.

My research studies the operations of on-demand delivery and mobility platforms. I combine data-driven optimization, econometric modeling, causal inference, and machine learning to understand platform decisions, driver behavior, customer behavior, and travel demand.
