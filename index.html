About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Data and Systems Engineering at The University of Hong Kong, working with Prof. Max Z.J. Shen and Prof. Shaochong Lin. I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from HKU in 2026, advised by Prof. Jintao Ke. I was fortunate to work with Prof. Sheng Liu during my visit to the University of Toronto.

My research focuses on the operations of on-demand delivery and mobility platforms. I use data-driven optimization to design and evaluate dispatching strategies, econometric and causal methods to understand driver and customer behavior, and machine learning and deep learning to forecast demand across locations and over time.
